Activity is combined across every episode and user. “Recent viewers” means users with activity within your selected inactivity window. Open “Viewer details” to see who watched and when. Keep marks a series you intend to retain; it is excluded from the stagnant size total.

New playback counts after 60 seconds of non-paused client reports. Earlier Jellyfin last-played dates are imported as “Existing Jellyfin history”; those dates can also reflect manual watched changes. Missing history means no recorded activity, not proof that a show was never watched. Observation starts when this plugin first sees a series.

*Size sums the episode file sizes known to Jellyfin. It excludes sidecar files and can omit alternate versions or extra parts. “Partial” means some sizes are missing. This is an estimate, not an exact folder total.
